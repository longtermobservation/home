Long-Term Observation: Humans

Long-Term Observation: Humans is an ongoing field guide series compiled under prolonged exposure conditions. It documents domestic systems, human social behavior, environmental inefficiencies, and the cumulative effects of gravity, responsibility, and unplanned emotional proximity.

What began as a private technical log intended to maintain objectivity during a short-term retirement has expanded into a multi-volume record of daily human life — including overloaded backpacks, shared meals, infrastructure failures, and the quiet weight people carry without formal acknowledgment.

These volumes are not intended as instruction manuals. No conclusions have been reached. Observation remains ongoing.
